About Us

Serving the Sea with Passion

Freya Yachting was founded in 2020 to provide the best service in sailing yacht charter and sailing lessons departing from Fethiye and Göcek.

We offer sailing yacht charter, sailing lessons, flotilla organisation, yacht management and maintenance services. We welcome our guests at Ecesaray Marina in Fethiye.
