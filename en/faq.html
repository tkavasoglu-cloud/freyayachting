Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you may want to know about yacht charter.

If you are not an experienced sailor, we offer charter with a skipper on board. If you hold a sufficient sailing licence, charter without a skipper is also possible. All our yachts other than Freya (the Beneteau Oceanis 423 and the Jeanneau Sun Odyssey 36i) can be chartered with or without a skipper; chartering without a skipper requires an ICC or equivalent certificate. Freya is chartered with a skipper only. See the yacht pages for details: Beneteau Oceanis 423, Jeanneau Sun Odyssey 36i.
Our prices include use of the yacht, basic safety equipment and tax. The skipper is included in the price on Freya; on the Beneteau Oceanis 423 and the Jeanneau Sun Odyssey 36i the skipper is optional and charged per day. On Freya, fuel within Göcek is included in the price; cleaning (150 EUR) and the transit log (100 EUR) are paid separately. If you sail outside Göcek, the yacht leaves with a full tank and the guest tops up the tank on return. Mooring buoy / mooring fees in the Göcek bays are not included in the price (about 70-80 EUR per night, per yacht, paid online by credit card via deria.gov.tr). Meals are not included in the price.
The Gulf of Fethiye and Göcek, Butterfly Valley, Gemiler Island and the Blue Voyage route are our main routes.
You can request a reservation through the reservation page on our website, by WhatsApp or by e-mail. You can also call us on +90 533 811 00 50.
Yes, a reservation is confirmed once the down payment is paid. The standard down payment is 50% of the rental fee. The down payment amount is communicated to you when you book; the remaining balance is paid on the first day of the charter.
In case of cancellation, the amount you paid is refunded according to the time remaining before the charter date: in full if 60 days or more remain; 50% if 30–59 days remain; no refund if fewer than 30 days remain.
The yacht is usually handed over on Saturday afternoons at Ecesaray Marina, Fethiye.
Light and comfortable clothes, sunscreen, sunglasses and non-slip shoes. Prefer a soft bag to a suitcase.
