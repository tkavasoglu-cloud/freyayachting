Your route, your call

Weekly skippered or bareboat charter: sail the yacht yourself or add a skipper by the day. Leave Ecesaray Marina, Fethiye, on Saturday at 15:00 and return the following Saturday at 09:00; the Göcek bays are yours for seven nights.

Weekly Rate (whole yacht)

May and October
1,800 EUR
June and September
2,000 EUR
July and August

The rate depends on the month of the Saturday the week starts on. The season runs from the first Saturday of May to the end of October.

Paid separately

  • Mooring buoys: approx. 70-80 EUR per night, paid in the bay
  • Skipper (skippered charter): 150 EUR per day
  • Optional petrol outboard for the tender: 250 EUR per week
  • Food (the yacht has a galley)

Licence and deposit

  • Bareboat: ICC or equivalent licence required
  • No licence? Charter with a skipper
  • Damage deposit 1,000 EUR, refunded if returned undamaged

Booking

  • On request: once the owner confirms, secured with a 50% down payment, balance on the first day
  • Cancellation: 60+ days full refund, 30-59 days 50%
  • VIP transfer complimentary for bookings made until 30 April 2027

Bareboat charter requires the person in charge of the yacht to hold an ICC (International Certificate of Competence) or an equivalent licence. No licence? Add a skipper for 150 EUR per day.

There is no instant booking for this yacht: we check weekly availability with the owner and get back to you. Just tell us which week you have in mind.
